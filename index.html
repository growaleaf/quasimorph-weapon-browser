Every weapon from the wiki's Weapons page in one sortable, filterable table, so you can compare damage type against a target's resistance without opening six separate category pages. This page does not rank or recommend any weapon — it is a browser, not a tier list. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; everything renders from the data in this page in your browser.

Armor Mitigation Estimator

Community-reported, unconfirmed (single source, patch-dependent). Both claims below come from the same Steam Community discussion thread, posted by two different community members — not from an official source, a patch note, or a second independent thread. A direct research pass on 2026-10-28 (WebSearch + direct reads of three other Quasimorph Steam threads and a third-party armor guide) found no second source for either claim, and no source anywhere that documents how a specific armor VALUE converts into a reduction percentage. Treat every number below as a reported floor, not a verified formula.

Claim 1 (the floor): "Armor doesn't negate the damage entirely, it can't reduce it bellow 5% (10% for assault rifles)." — phoagne, Steam Community, Jul 13 2024.
Claim 2 (the ordering, not used in the arithmetic below): "Damage taken multiplier applies before damage reduction by armor." — GreatPalm, same thread. Source thread.

No source found gives a formula for how an armor VALUE maps to a reduction percentage — only this floor. So this estimator cannot compute "post-armor damage for armor value N"; it can only show the floor-protected range a hit's damage cannot be reduced below, regardless of how high the target's armor is, using the weapon's own min/max damage from the table above.
